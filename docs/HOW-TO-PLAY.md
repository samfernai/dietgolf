# How to play Diet Golf

Seven holes, one a day, scored on the calories you eat against the calories you
need. It is a calorie tracker for people who would rather not be told they are
using a calorie tracker.

## The whole game in one hole

A Tuesday. Par 4. Maintenance 2,118.

| Time | Shot | What happened | Calories |
| ---- | ---- | ------------- | -------- |
| 08:00 | | Porridge and berries | +450 |
| 13:00 | | Burger and chips | +980 |
| 14:31 | | Bike ride | −620 |
| | | Your body needed | −2,118 |
| | | **Net balance** | **−1,308** |

A balance between −1,000 and −1,999 is a birdie. On a par 4 that is a **3**.

## 1. Getting on the course

Tap **Register and tee off**. It asks for two things on the first screen — the
name you want on the leaderboard and a four-digit PIN — and then for your height,
weight, age and sex.

**There is no "forgot my PIN".** None. Pick one you will remember, and please do
not reuse the one from your bank card.

Those measurements are not decoration. They work out the calories your body needs
to hold its weight, which is the line every hole is scored against. The app uses
Mifflin-St Jeor at a sedentary baseline, because the exercise you log gets
subtracted separately — otherwise your bike ride would count twice.

You can change any of it later on the **You** tab. Days you have already closed
out keep the figures you played them off.

## 2. Playing a hole

Each day is one hole, and it opens when the day does. The **Play** tab lands on
today.

Log what you eat under **Breakfast**, **Lunch**, **Dinner**, **Drinks** or
**Other** — a calorie figure, a time, and a note if the story deserves one. Log
what you burn under **Exercise**, with what you did to earn it. You can put in as
many entries per meal as you like; a day is rarely one sitting.

**The hole only counts once you close the day out.** That button is the whole
honesty system: without it, logging breakfast and then forgetting about it would
read as a 2,000-calorie deficit and an eagle.

## 3. Shots are played against the clock

The hole is checked through the day, and the shot tracker moves as you log.

| Par | Checkpoints |
| --- | ----------- |
| 3 | 10:00, 20:00 |
| 4 | 10:00, 14:00, 20:00 |
| 5 | 10:00, 14:00, 18:00, 21:00 |

Your maintenance is spread across the day, so a checkpoint asks whether you are
**on pace right now** — not whether you have already eaten a full day's worth.
Without that, every breakfast would read as an albatross.

At midnight the hole settles on the full day. This is why a hole can show a
birdie at 8pm and come back to a par: the evening's remaining maintenance has
not accrued yet when the 8pm shot is played.

## 4. The scoring bands

Net balance means intake, minus exercise, minus maintenance.

| Score | vs par | Net calories |
| ----- | ------ | ------------ |
| Albatross | −3 | −3,500 or lower *(par 3 only — it aces the hole)* |
| Eagle | −2 | −2,000 or lower *(no eagle on a par 3)* |
| Birdie | −1 | −1,000 to −1,999 |
| Par | level | −999 to +250 |
| Bogey | +1 | +251 to +1,500 |
| Double bogey | +2 | +1,501 to +2,750 |
| Triple bogey | +3 | +2,751 to +3,500 |
| Snowman | +4 | +3,501 and above |

**Women play off bands 15% tighter** — a birdie at −850 rather than −1,000. The
same deficit is a larger share of a smaller maintenance, so the grades move in
proportion. "Rather not say" uses the base table.

**A par 3 has no eagle.** One under a par 3 is a birdie and two under is the hole
itself, so the eagle band folds into the birdie and only an albatross-sized
deficit aces it.

**The two caps.** A hole is never better than two under par, and never worse than
six over, however the day went.

**No returns.** A day in the past you never closed out goes down as five over par
— worse than almost any day you could honestly log. Owning up to a shocker costs
you less than quietly closing the app.

A word on the deep end: the lowest bands are deliberately hard to reach. A −3,500
day is roughly a pound of fat and is not meant as a daily target. It exists so
that an albatross stays as rare as it should be.

## 5. The course and the tournament

The shape of the week never changes, so scores mean the same thing from one week
to the next.

| Hole | Day | Par | S.I. | |
| ---- | --- | --- | ---- | --- |
| 1 | Monday | 4 | 7 | |
| 2 | Tuesday | 4 | 6 | |
| 3 | Wednesday | 5 | 5 | |
| 4 | Thursday | 5 | 3 | |
| 5 | Friday | 4 | 2 | **Amen Corner** |
| 6 | Saturday | 5 | 1 | **Amen Corner** |
| 7 | Sunday | 3 | 4 | **Amen Corner** |
| | **Total** | **30** | | |

Each Monday the course takes its identity from a PGA Tour event — the name, the
host course and where it is. The pars and stroke indexes stay put, because those
are what let you compare this week with last. Masters week gets Augusta's real
hole names; everywhere else the holes are named from the plant pool.

**Amen Corner** is Friday, Saturday and Sunday: the stretch that decides the
week, and the stretch that contains every social occasion you have ever been
invited to.

## 6. Handicaps

Enter an ordinary 18-hole handicap, 0 to 54. It is scaled to this seven-hole
course and the shots go out hardest hole first by stroke index — your first lands
on Saturday, your second on Friday.

An 18 handicapper gets a shot a hole. Someone off 8 gets three, on Saturday,
Friday and Thursday.

**Everyone can see your handicap next to your name.** Over seven holes,
sandbagging is extremely obvious.

## 7. The leaderboard

Live all week, refreshing about every twenty seconds. "Thru 3" means three holes
have counted so far.

- **Gross** — your raw score against par.
- **Net** — gross minus your handicap shots. The default, and the fairest fight.
- **Points** — Stableford: two for a net par, one more for each shot better,
  nothing for a net double bogey or worse.

Tap anyone to see their full card — every meal, every calorie, every note.
**Your log is not private.** Write accordingly.

## 8. Statistics

The **Stats** tab is modelled on the PGA Tour's. None of these have a natural
meaning in a calorie game, so each one is defined explicitly and the definition
sits beside the number in the app.

| Stat | What it means here |
| ---- | ------------------ |
| Scoring average | Mean strokes per hole played. |
| Driving distance | How far the 10:00 checkpoint got you, scaled so a day on pace is 290 yards. Par 4s and 5s only. |
| Fairways in regulation | Par 4s and 5s where you were on pace or better at 10:00. |
| Greens in regulation | Holes where you were on pace at the shot that has to find the green — 10:00 on a par 3, 14:00 on a par 4, 18:00 on a par 5. |
| Scrambling | Of the greens you missed, how often you still made par or better. |
| Putts per round | Strokes taken after reaching the green, scaled to seven holes. |
| SG: Off the tee | Your 10:00 grade against everyone else who played that hole that week. |
| SG: Approach to green | Your mid-day grades against the same field. |
| SG: Putting | Your putts against the field's on the same hole. |
| One-putt percentage | Holes finished with a single putt. |
| Putting inside 10 feet | When the approach left you under par pace, how often you converted in one. |
| 3-putt avoidance | Holes that did not run to three putts or more. |
| Daily scoring average | Your mean score on each day of the week. |

Strokes gained needs someone else to have played the same hole — with nobody to
measure against it stays blank rather than inventing a baseline.

## 9. Local rules

1. **The day ends at midnight**, in the group's timezone.
2. **No playing ahead.** Thursday's hole is locked until Thursday.
3. **Backfilling within the week is legal.** Filling Monday in on Wednesday is
   fine. Filling the whole week in on Sunday night is legal too, but everyone
   will know.
4. **Sunday midnight closes the card.** Last week's scores are frozen, and
   Monday's course is already open.
5. **The app cannot tell whether you logged everything.** The group can. That is
   the only anti-cheat this game has, and it is the correct amount.
6. **Play it as it lies.** If you are guessing at the calories, guess high.

## 10. For the non-golfers

| Term | Means |
| ---- | ----- |
| Par | The score a hole is supposed to take. Level par is a good week. |
| Birdie | One better than par. |
| Eagle | Two better than par. |
| Bogey | One worse than par. Perfectly survivable. |
| Snowman | An 8. On this course's par 4s, literally. |
| Stroke index | How hard a hole is, 1 to 7. Decides who gets shots where. |
| Stableford | A points format that ignores disasters. Two points is a net par. |
| In regulation | Reaching the green with two shots left for putting. |
| Scrambling | Making par after missing the green. The art of getting away with it. |
| Amen Corner | At Augusta, the three holes that decide the Masters. Here, the weekend. |

## The instruction sheet

**Every day**

1. Open **Play**. It lands on today.
2. Log what you ate, with rough calories and a time.
3. Log anything you burned.
4. **Close out the day.** The hole does not count until you do.

**The maths** — intake, minus exercise, minus the calories you need. Near zero is
a par. A thousand under is a birdie. Two thousand under is an eagle.

**The clock** — holes are checked at 10:00 and 20:00 on a par 3; 10:00, 14:00 and
20:00 on a par 4; 10:00, 14:00, 18:00 and 21:00 on a par 5. Midnight settles it.

**The week** — Mon 4, Tue 4, Wed 5, Thu 5, Fri 4, Sat 5, Sun 3. Par 30. Amen
Corner is Fri–Sun. New course and new tournament every Monday.
