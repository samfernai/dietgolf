# How to play Diet Golf

Seven holes, one a day. Everything you eat is a shot. It is a diet tracker for
people who would rather not be told they are using a diet tracker.

> A designed version of this page, with a printable instruction sheet, lives at
> the handbook link in the project README.

## The whole game in one hole

A Monday. Par 4.

| | Shot | What happened | Strokes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Breakfast, the tee shot | Porridge with fruit — striped down the middle | −1 |
| 2 | Lunch, the second shot | Pizza — blocked into the trees on the right | +2 |
| 3 | Dinner, the approach | Healthy chicken dinner — recovered to the green | −1 |
| 4 | Drinks, the putt | One glass of wine — lipped out the par putt | +1 |
| | | **Par 4, plus one** | **5 — bogey** |

## 1. Getting on the course

Tap **Register and tee off** and give it two things: the name you want on the
leaderboard, and a four-digit PIN. That is the whole sign-up — no email, no
password, no verification link sitting in a spam folder.

**There is no "forgot my PIN".** None. Pick one you will remember, and please do
not reuse the one from your bank card.

It will also ask for a handicap. If you have a real one, use it. If you don't, 18
is the honest middle, and you can change it later.

## 2. Playing a hole

Each day is one hole, and it opens when the day does. The **Play** tab always
lands on today. You play up to five shots, in the order a day actually happens:

| What you log | The shot it is | Roughly |
| ------------ | -------------- | ------- |
| Breakfast | Tee shot | Sets up the hole |
| Lunch | Second shot | Where most days are won or lost |
| Dinner | Approach | Your chance to recover |
| Snacks | Short game | The bit nobody practises |
| Drinks | The putt | The most treacherous shot in golf |

Tap a meal, pick one of five ratings, and add a note if the story deserves one —
*"hit one into the trees, as I had a burger for lunch"*. Notes run to 280
characters, which is more than enough to explain yourself.

**You do not have to log all five.** Log what actually happened. A day with three
entries is a perfectly good hole. Change or delete any of them while the week is
open.

Your shots appear on a drawn map of the hole as you go, so the shape of the day
is visible at a glance: a good one marches up the middle, a bad one zig-zags
through the trees.

## 3. The five ratings

Every shot is rated on the same scale, whatever meal it was. The wording changes
to suit the meal; the maths never does.

| Rating | What it means | Strokes |
| ------ | ------------- | ------- |
| 🎯 Striped it | Genuinely good. The choice you'd happily mention to someone. | −1 |
| ⛳ Fairway | Sensible. Nothing to report either way. | level |
| 🌾 Rough | A bit more than you meant. Still playable. | +1 |
| 🌲 Trees | Takeaway, big plate, seconds. You know the one. | +2 |
| 💦 Water | A genuine disaster. You will not need to ask which of these it was. | +3 |

## 4. Doing the maths

Start at the hole's par. Add up your ratings. That's your score.

So on a par 4, five sensible fairways is a **par**. One trip to the trees is a
**bogey**. A striped breakfast that offsets a rough evening is **level**.

**The two caps**

- **Nothing better than two under par.** So Sunday's par 3 can be holed in one,
  but an albatross is off the table.
- **Nothing worse than six over par.** However the day went. Pick up, write the
  number down, walk to the next tee.

**No returns**

A day in the past with *nothing at all* on it is a **no return**, and it goes
down as **five over par**. That is deliberately worse than almost any day you
could honestly log. Owning up to a shocker costs you less than quietly closing
the app, which is the entire point.

You can still fill it in — a no return is only final when the week ends on Sunday
night. Until then, Monday is sitting there waiting for you.

## 5. The course

The shape of the week never changes, so scores mean the same thing from one week
to the next. A new course — new name, new hole names, new yardages, newly drawn
holes — opens every Monday morning.

| Hole | Day | Par | S.I. | |
| ---- | --- | --- | ---- | --- |
| 1 | Monday | 4 | 7 | |
| 2 | Tuesday | 4 | 6 | |
| 3 | Wednesday | 5 | 5 | |
| 4 | Thursday | 5 | 3 | |
| 5 | Friday | 4 | 2 | **Amen Corner** |
| 6 | Saturday | 5 | 1 | **Amen Corner** |
| 7 | Sunday | 3 | 4 | **Amen Corner** |
| | **Total** | **30** | | |

S.I. is the stroke index — 1 is the hardest hole, 7 the easiest.

**Amen Corner** is Friday, Saturday and Sunday: the stretch that decides the
week, and the stretch that contains every social occasion you have ever been
invited to. Saturday is stroke index 1 for a reason.

## 6. Handicaps

Enter an ordinary 18-hole handicap, anywhere from 0 to 54. The app scales it to
this seven-hole course and hands the shots out hardest hole first, by stroke
index — so your first shot lands on Saturday, your second on Friday, and so on.

An 18 handicapper gets a shot a hole. Someone off 8 gets three shots, on
Saturday, Friday and Thursday. The point is that a 24 has a real game against a
scratch player.

Change it whenever you like on the **You** tab. It updates the current week.
Finished weeks keep the handicap you actually played them off, so you can't
rewrite history.

**Everyone can see your handicap next to your name.** Over seven holes,
sandbagging is extremely obvious.

## 7. The leaderboard

Live all week, refreshing about every twenty seconds. "Thru 3" means three holes
have counted so far. Three ways to read it:

- **Gross** — your raw score against par. No handicap, no mercy.
- **Net** — gross minus your handicap shots. This is the default, and the fairest
  fight.
- **Points** — Stableford: two points for a net par, one more for each shot
  better, nothing for a net double bogey or worse. It rewards a few good days and
  forgives one catastrophe.

Tap anyone to see their full card, hole by hole, shot by shot, notes and all. The
**Season** tab keeps running totals across every week you've played.

**Your notes are not private.** Everyone in the game can read them. Write
accordingly.

## 8. Reading a card

The scorecard uses the notation a real one does, so it can be read at a glance.

| Mark | Means |
| ---- | ----- |
| Double ring, red | Eagle or better. Rare. Screenshot it. |
| Single ring, red | Birdie. One under par. |
| No mark | Par. A good, quiet day. |
| Single square | Bogey. One over. |
| Double square | Double bogey or worse. |
| Dashed ring, greyed | A no return. Nothing was logged. |

## 9. Local rules

1. **The day ends at midnight, UK time.** Wherever you happen to be standing.
   (Set `APP_TIMEZONE` if your group lives somewhere else.)
2. **No playing ahead.** Thursday's hole is locked until Thursday. Optimism is
   not a score.
3. **Backfilling within the week is legal.** Filling Monday in on Wednesday is
   fine. Filling the whole week in on Sunday night is legal too, but everyone
   will know.
4. **Sunday midnight closes the card.** Last week's scores are frozen, and
   Monday's course is already open.
5. **Nothing stops you logging one virtuous porridge and closing the app.** The
   scoring can't tell. The group can. That is the only anti-cheat this game has,
   and it is the correct amount.
6. **Play it as it lies.** If you're wondering whether that was rough or trees,
   it was trees.

## 10. For the non-golfers

| Term | Means |
| ---- | ----- |
| Par | The score a hole is supposed to take. Level par is a good week. |
| Birdie | One better than par. |
| Eagle | Two better than par. The best score available here. |
| Bogey | One worse than par. Perfectly survivable. |
| Stroke index | How hard a hole is, 1 to 7. Decides who gets shots where. |
| Stableford | A points format that ignores disasters. Two points is a net par. |
| Amen Corner | At Augusta, the three holes that decide the Masters. Here, the weekend. |
| Lipped out | The ball went round the edge of the hole and stayed out. Deeply unfair. |

## The instruction sheet

**Every day**

1. Open **Play**. It lands on today.
2. Tap each meal you had.
3. Pick one of five ratings.
4. Add the note. The note is the fun bit.

**The ratings** — striped −1, fairway level, rough +1, trees +2, water +3.

**The maths** — par, plus your ratings. Best possible is two under; worst is six
over. A day left blank is a no return, worth five over — worse than owning up to
almost anything.

**The week** — Mon 4, Tue 4, Wed 5, Thu 5, Fri 4, Sat 5, Sun 3. Par 30. Amen
Corner is Fri–Sun. New course every Monday. The board is live; the card closes
Sunday midnight.
